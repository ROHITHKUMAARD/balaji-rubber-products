Welcome to Balaji Rubber Products. Established in 2003 in Bengaluru, Karnataka, we are a leading ISO 9001 and MSME-registered manufacturer of precision-engineered industrial rubber components, high-temperature silicone profiles, Viton (FKM) chemical-resistant seals, nitrile (NBR) oil-resistant parts, and durable castable polyurethane products.

Industrial Rubber Solutions and Products Portfolio

We design, mould, and manufacture critical elastomeric solutions for OEMs, automotive assemblies, hydraulic systems, pneumatic machinery, and agricultural processors across India:

  • Metal Bonded Rubber Products - Anti-vibration mounts and engine damping solutions.
  • Rubber Bellows - Flexible expansion joints and dust protection for industrial machinery.
  • Rubber Seals & O-Rings - Precision sealing rings manufactured in NBR, EPDM, Viton, and Silicone.
  • Rubber Diaphragms - Precision moulded flexible membranes for pumps, valves, and actuators.
  • Nitrile Rubber (NBR) Products - Oil, fuel, and chemical resistant sealing components.
  • Rubber Rice Rollers - High-performance rollers engineered for rice milling and dehusking.
  • Silicone Rubber Products - High-temperature, food-grade, and pharmaceutical grade profiles.
  • Silicone Tubes - Flexible transparent tubing for medical and fluid transfer systems.
  • Silicone O-Rings - Heat and weather-resistant elastomeric sealing rings.
  • Viton Rubber Products - Acid, fuel, and extreme chemical-resistant FKM seals.
  • Viton O-Rings - High-temperature sealing solutions for chemical and petroleum industries.
  • Polyurethane Products - High-abrasion and wear-resistant castable PU components.
  • Polyurethane Rollers - Heavy load-bearing industrial rollers for material handling.
