Industrial Compounding & Material Formulations

Different industrial applications require specific elastomeric compounds tailored for temperature, chemical exposure, tensile strength, and environmental conditions. Balaji Rubber Products specializes in formulating and processing the following high-grade polymers:

  • Nitrile Rubber (NBR / Buna-N): Formulated specifically for petroleum oils, mineral oils, hydraulic fluids, and diesel fuels. Operating range: -30°C to +120°C. Ideal for O-rings, hydraulic packings, automotive fuel seals, and oil pumps.
  • Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer (EPDM): Outstanding resistance to outdoor weathering, ozone, UV exposure, hot water, and steam. Excellent electrical insulating properties. Frequently utilized for automotive weatherstripping, window seals, outdoor gaskets, and HVAC piping.
  • Fluoroelastomer (Viton / FKM): Designed for extreme operational environments involving aggressive acids, chemical solvents, synthetic lubricants, and high heat up to +250°C. Widely specified in aerospace, defense, chemical processing, and petroleum refining equipment.
  • Silicone Rubber (VMQ): Physiologically inert, non-toxic, odorless, and suitable for food contact (FDA compliant) and medical devices. Maintains flexibility across extreme thermal extremes from -60°C to +230°C. Utilized for food processing machinery, autoclaves, and pharmaceutical fluid handling.
  • Neoprene (Chloroprene / CR): Provides balanced resistance to mechanical abrasion, weathering, moderate oils, and flame. Excellent general-purpose engineering elastomer for industrial machinery, vibration pads, and marine equipment.
  • Natural Rubber (NR): Superior dynamic fatigue resistance, tear strength, high tensile resilience, and low compression set. Extensively applied in heavy engine mountings, anti-vibration bushes, and shock absorbers.
  • Castable Polyurethane (PU): Bridges the gap between rubber elasticity and plastic toughness. Provides unmatched abrasion resistance, load-bearing capacity, and cut resistance. Engineered for heavy industrial slurry handling, conveyor scrapers, mining sieves, and polyurethane drive rollers.
